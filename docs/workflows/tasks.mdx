Tasks groups obligations by who owes the next move, what changed, and what proves completion.

<Frame caption="Open, waiting, and done with counterparty, evidence, and closure receipts visible.">
  <img src="/images/product/tasks-obligations.png" alt="ubik Tasks board grouped by open, waiting, and completed obligations" />
</Frame>

<Columns cols={4}>
  <Card title="Open" icon="circle-dot">Work owed by you or your team.</Card>
  <Card title="Waiting" icon="hourglass">Work owed by a supplier, buyer, or another owner.</Card>
  <Card title="Probably closed" icon="scan-search">Work whose evidence suggests it may already be complete.</Card>
  <Card title="Closed today" icon="badge-check">Completed work with a reason or receipt.</Card>
</Columns>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter">Choose Mine or Team, then Open, Waiting, Probably closed, Closed today, or All.</Step>
  <Step title="Group">Use list, board, or category to compare urgency, owner, source, and counterparty.</Step>
  <Step title="Inspect">Check the source, confidence signals, receipts, activity, and next action.</Step>
  <Step title="Close">Record the reason or receipt that proves completion.</Step>
</Steps>

<div className="ubik-panel">External owners can act by email. They do not need a ubik seat.</div>
