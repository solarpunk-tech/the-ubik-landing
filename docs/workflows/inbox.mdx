Inbox keeps the source message central. Context and actions stay close without replacing the conversation.

<Frame caption="Evidence-first Inbox: thread list, source message, context tabs, and operator actions in one view.">
  <img src="/images/product/inbox-evidence-first.png" alt="ubik Inbox showing a rate-confirmation thread, its source messages, context tabs, and reply actions" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Triage the queue">Use Business critical, Action required, Waiting on, Updates, and All mail to narrow the work.</Step>
  <Step title="Read the thread">Check the message and quoted history before its summary.</Step>
  <Step title="Open context">Use Tasks, Waiting for, Files, and Links only when they change the decision.</Step>
  <Step title="Move">Reply, assign, ask ubik, or wait for the missing owner or evidence.</Step>
</Steps>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Source boundary" icon="eye">ubik reads approved inboxes and connected sources. Customer data does not train a model.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Action boundary" icon="user-check">Replies, approvals, assignments, and writebacks wait for review.</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
