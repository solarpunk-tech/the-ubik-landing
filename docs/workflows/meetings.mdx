## The meeting loop

<Steps>
  <Step title="Capture">Record a call when it may change price, timing, supplier reliability, customer expectations, or ownership.</Step>
  <Step title="Review">Correct the prepared notes and confirm the decisions, owners, follow-ups, and unresolved questions.</Step>
  <Step title="Attach">Link the meeting to the account, supplier, shipment, project, or workspace context it changed.</Step>
  <Step title="Follow through">Ask later what was promised and confirm that the task queue reflects the commitment.</Step>
</Steps>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where does the recording live?" icon="hard-drive" defaultOpen>
    Recordings stay on the operator's device unless they are deliberately attached to the workspace.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What should I check before sharing notes?" icon="shield-check">
    Review names, commitments, prices, sensitive context, owners, and anything inferred from an unclear part of the conversation.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
