<Update label="October 15, 2026" description="Document Verification, rebuilt" tags={["Document Verification", "CEO Panel", "Capture", "Planning"]}>
  ## Document Verification

  - Review every document in a shipment in one place, and see why each one passed or failed.
  - Admins choose who can verify and approve, under **Access**.

  ## CEO Panel

  - Ask a question or leave a comment for your team from any view.

  ## Capture

  - Pick the report and the day before you scan.

  ## Planning

  - Planning reports can now arrive by email.

  ## Faster

  - ubik opens faster, and saving no longer reloads the page.
</Update>

<Update label="October 13, 2026" description="Fewer sign-outs, clearer CEO Panel numbers, packaging containers" tags={["Sign-in", "CEO Panel", "Packaging Tracker", "Production Reconciliation"]}>
  ## Sign-in

  - You stay signed in on slow connections.

  ## CEO Panel

  - See how fresh each number is, and click a price to see its lines.

  ## Packaging PO

  - Containers keep inner and outer packs apart, and take notes.

  ## Production Reconciliation

  - Production dates open without a blank page.
</Update>

<Update label="October 10, 2026" description="Instant Packaging Tracker buttons" tags={["Packaging Tracker", "Mobile"]}>
  ## Packaging Tracker

  - Buttons respond instantly.

  ## Mobile

  - Dictate to Ask ubik on iOS and Android.
</Update>

<Update label="October 9, 2026" description="Supplier weekly report and previous orders" tags={["Supplier Update", "Packaging Tracker", "CEO Panel"]}>
  ## Supplier Update

  - Download a weekly report in Excel.
  - Ask about any row; the separate Accept step is gone.

  ## Packaging PO

  - Check artwork against the previous order, shown under each line.

  ## CEO Panel

  - A source that fails now says so.
</Update>

<Update label="October 6, 2026" description="Packaging Tracker: why this status" tags={["Packaging Tracker", "Replenishment", "CEO Panel", "Mobile"]}>
  ## Packaging Tracker

  - Every task says why it has its status.
  - Chase waiting tasks, close them in bulk, and reopen closed ones.

  ## Replenishment

  - Pick warehouses, customers and products by name.

  ## CEO Panel

  - Switch between French and English from the header.

  ## Mobile

  - Dictation no longer crashes the iOS app.
</Update>
