## Signing in with Google

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the ubik login page">Open the ubik sign-in screen.</Step>
  <Step title='Click "Continue with Google"'>The browser goes straight to Google's sign-in screen.</Step>
  <Step title="Sign into your Google account">Choose an already-signed-in account, or enter your credentials.</Step>
  <Step title="Approve access (first time only)">Google shows a basic consent screen — name, email, profile picture. Click **Allow**.</Step>
  <Step title="You're back in ubik">You're redirected back into ubik, already signed in.</Step>
</Steps>

<Card title="Connect Gmail as a source" icon="plug" href="/admin/integrations">
  Once signed in, connect Gmail under Integrations so ubik can read buyer inquiries, supplier replies, attachments, and approvals.
</Card>
