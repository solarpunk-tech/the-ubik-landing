## The loop

<div className="ubik-steps">
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>01 · Read</span> Start with the latest message and its quoted history.</div>
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>02 · Check</span> Open Summary, Tasks, Files, or Related when needed.</div>
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>03 · Act</span> Reply, reply all, forward, assign, or wait.</div>
</div>

<div className="ubik-panel">
  ubik reads approved inboxes. Replies and writebacks wait for human review. Customer data does not train a model.
