## Daily operating rhythm

<div className="ubik-steps">
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>01 · Triage risk</span> Start with work that can affect customer promises, margin, clearance, cash timing, or supplier reliability.</div>
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>02 · Inspect evidence</span> Check the source thread, document, meeting, task, or system field before the recommendation becomes operating state.</div>
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>03 · Decide ownership</span> Approve, edit, assign, wait, or escalate based on who has authority to move the work.</div>
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>04 · Leave a trail</span> Preserve what changed, who reviewed it, what moved, and what still needs a decision.</div>
</div>
