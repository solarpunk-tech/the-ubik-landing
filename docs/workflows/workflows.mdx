The current shipping motion uses the same primitives - Ask ubik, Inbox, Tasks, Meetings - composed by hand. Workflows turns those compositions into named, reusable playbooks with approval gates and audit trails.

## What we are building

- Visual builder for chaining agents across inbox, meetings, tasks, and ERP.
- Approval gates at every commercial, finance, customer, or system-writeback decision point.
- Per-workflow memory, evidence rules, and audit trail.
- Templates for seafood-native loops: RFQ to quote, PO ingestion, transit-aware scheduling, VMI pull-rate, and lot traceability.

Until Workflows ships, the [daily operating queue](/workflows/daily-operating-queue) and [Ask ubik](/workflows/ask-ubik) carry the same outcome, just operator-composed.
