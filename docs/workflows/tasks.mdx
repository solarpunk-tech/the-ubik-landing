Tasks lets you turn any inbox reply, meeting follow-up, or agent recommendation into a clear action with an owner, a deadline, and a trail. It is built individual-first — your own queue first, then the team.

## What Tasks does

| Surface | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Personal queue | Tasks you own — sorted by deadline, source, and what is blocked. |
| Assign to anyone | A teammate on ubik gets the task in their queue. A teammate not on ubik gets an email with the task and can reply. |
| Source attached | Every task carries the email, meeting, or thread it came from. |
| Activity trail | Reassignments, edits, completions — all timestamped. |

## A typical task loop

<div className="ubik-steps">
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>01 · Create</span> From an inbox thread, a meeting note, or an agent recommendation — turn it into a task in one click.</div>
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>02 · Assign</span> Keep it. Hand it to a teammate. Or send it to a supplier / customer / forwarder who isn't on ubik — they receive an email with the task.</div>
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>03 · Track</span> The owner sees it in their queue (or inbox, if they're external). You see the status from yours.</div>
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>04 · Close</span> Mark done, edit, or reassign. The trail stays attached to the source.</div>
</div>

<div className="ubik-panel">
  <strong>Share without seats.</strong> You do not need a teammate on ubik to assign them work. The email-based handoff means freight forwarders, suppliers, and customers can act on your task and reply without onboarding into a new tool.
</div>

## Useful prompts around tasks

| Situation | Ask ubik |
| --- | --- |
| End of day | "Which of my tasks are blocked waiting on someone else?" |
| Manager view | "Which tasks have been open more than 5 days and what is blocking them?" |
| Supplier chase | "Create a task to chase the freight forwarder for the BL-2408-219 update." |
| Customer follow-up | "Turn this buyer ask into a task for me, attach the last quote." |
