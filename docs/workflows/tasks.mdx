Tasks groups obligations by who owes the next move, what changed, and what proves completion.

<Frame caption="Open, waiting, and done with counterparty, evidence, and closure receipts visible.">
  <img src="/images/product/tasks-obligations.png" alt="ubik Tasks board grouped by open, waiting, and completed obligations" />
</Frame>

| State | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| Open | Your team owes the next move |
| Waiting | A buyer, supplier, or another owner owes it |
| Probably closed | Evidence suggests the work moved |
| Closed today | A reason or receipt proves completion |

<div className="ubik-panel">External owners can act by email. They do not need a ubik seat.</div>
