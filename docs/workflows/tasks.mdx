Tasks turns an email, meeting note, approval, or agent recommendation into owned work. Each task keeps its source, owner, deadline, and activity.

## The loop

<div className="ubik-steps">
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>01 · Create</span> Turn evidence into a clear outcome.</div>
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>02 · Assign</span> Keep it, hand it to a teammate, or email an external owner.</div>
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>03 · Track</span> See whether evidence, approval, or a counterparty blocks it.</div>
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>04 · Close</span> Preserve the source and final decision.</div>
</div>

<div className="ubik-panel">
  External owners can act by email. They do not need a ubik seat.
</div>
