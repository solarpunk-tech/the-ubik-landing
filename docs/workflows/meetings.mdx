Most trade decisions get made in conversations that never reach the ERP — supplier sync calls, internal pricing reviews, customer escalations, logistics check-ins. Meetings keeps every one of them as part of your operating memory.

## What Meetings does

| Surface | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| On-device recorder | Captures the call locally first. No cloud upload until you approve. |
| Auto notes | Summary, decisions, owners, follow-ups — drafted as the call ends. |
| Trade memory link | Decisions are attached to the account, supplier, lane, or shipment they touched. |
| Searchable later | Ask ubik to recover what was said, who agreed, and what happened next. |

## A typical meeting loop

<div className="ubik-steps">
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>01 · Capture</span> Start the recorder before the call. It runs on your machine, not on a server.</div>
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>02 · Review</span> When the call ends, ubik drafts the summary, decisions, owners, and follow-ups.</div>
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>03 · Attach</span> Link the notes to the account, supplier, or shipment the conversation touched.</div>
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>04 · Recover</span> Ask ubik later: "What did the supplier commit to in the May review?"</div>
</div>

<div className="ubik-panel">
  <strong>Local-first by default.</strong> Recordings live on the operator's device unless you choose to attach them to the workspace. Sensitive supplier pricing and margin context stays under operator control.
</div>

## Useful prompts after a meeting

| Situation | Ask ubik |
| --- | --- |
| Decision recovery | "What pricing did the supplier commit to in the May 12 review?" |
| Owner check | "Who owns the FDA-hold follow-up from yesterday's call?" |
| Follow-up draft | "Draft the recap email for the freight forwarder sync." |
| Account memory | "Show every meeting where this buyer's floor price came up." |
