## Week 1: map

Shubhranshu and Hemanth map two or three workflows across the people who supply the input, check it, act on exceptions, and own the result. The first map records the current source, decision rule, review boundary, handoff, and failure state.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Map">Trace two or three loops across sales, operations, logistics, finance, documentation, and quality.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose">Pick the loop where source data, owner, and outcome can be checked.</Step>
  <Step title="Run for one week">Connect the required systems and put each customer promise or system write behind review.</Step>
  <Step title="Update with founders">Review misses and edge cases with Shubhranshu and Hemanth, then revise the workflow contract.</Step>
  <Step title="Measure">Compare the agreed baseline with the live result: response time, overdue work, SLA misses, review load, or another metric chosen for that workflow.</Step>
</Steps>
