Buyer RFQs, supplier replies, ETA changes, and finance approvals arrive in Inbox. ubik ranks the threads that can change a promise, margin, owner, or system state.

## The loop

<div className="ubik-steps">
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>01 · Detect</span> Find the thread that can change a promise, margin, or owner.</div>
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>02 · Recall</span> Attach quotes, inventory, lane history, and prior decisions.</div>
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>03 · Decide</span> Approve, edit, assign, wait, escalate, or reject.</div>
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>04 · Record</span> Keep the source, reviewer, and timestamp with the action.</div>
</div>

<div className="ubik-panel">
  ubik reads approved inboxes. Replies and writebacks wait for human review. Customer data does not train a model.
</div>

## Useful prompts

| Need | Ask ubik |
| --- | --- |
| Triage | "Which buyer or supplier threads have waited more than 36 hours?" |
| Quote | "Pull the last three quotes, current margin rule, and open inventory." |
| Review | "Who owns the next decision, and what evidence is missing?" |
