Tasks is an obligation view, not a folder tree. It shows who owes the next move, what evidence supports the state, how urgent the work is, and why a task was closed.

<Columns cols={4}>
  <Card title="Open" icon="circle-dot">Work owed by you or your team.</Card>
  <Card title="Waiting" icon="hourglass">Work owed by a supplier, buyer, or another owner.</Card>
  <Card title="Probably closed" icon="scan-search">Work whose evidence suggests it may already be complete.</Card>
  <Card title="Closed today" icon="badge-check">Completed work with a reason or receipt.</Card>

## Review a task

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter the obligation">Start with Mine or Team, then choose Open, Waiting, Probably closed, Closed today, or All.</Step>
  <Step title="Group the queue">Switch between list, board, and category views to see urgency, status, counterparty, source, or assignee.</Step>
  <Step title="Inspect the source and evidence">Check the thread, meeting, document, confidence signals, receipts, activity, and recommended next action.</Step>
  <Step title="Close with a reason">Record why the work is complete so the final state can be trusted later.</Step>
</Steps>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What does owed by them mean?" icon="users">
    The obligation is still open, but the next move belongs to a third party. ubik watches for the reply or evidence that changes the state.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is a closure receipt?" icon="receipt-text">
    A source-backed signal—such as an outbound reply, document, payment, invoice, or workflow closure—that shows the work actually moved.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can an external owner act without a seat?" icon="mail-check">
    Yes. External owners can receive and respond to an email handoff without joining the ubik workspace.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
