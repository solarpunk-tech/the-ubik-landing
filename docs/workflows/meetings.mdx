A reviewed meeting keeps the decision, owner, follow-up, and missing evidence searchable.

<Frame caption="Meeting notes, decisions, owners, and follow-ups stay connected to the workspace.">
  <img src="/images/product/meetings-workspace.jpg" alt="ubik Meetings workspace with recorded conversations and prepared notes" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Capture">Record on the operator's device.</Step>
  <Step title="Review">Correct decisions, owners, follow-ups, prices, and unclear passages.</Step>
  <Step title="Attach">Link approved notes to the account, supplier, shipment, or project they changed.</Step>
  <Step title="Carry">Create the work that must remain visible after the call.</Step>
</Steps>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Recording boundary" icon="hard-drive" defaultOpen>Recordings stay on the device until the operator attaches them.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Review boundary" icon="shield-check">Names, commitments, prices, owners, and inferred text need review before sharing.</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
