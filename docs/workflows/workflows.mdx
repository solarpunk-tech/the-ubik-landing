## Week 1

Shubhranshu and Hemanth map two or three loops across the people who supply the input, check it, act on exceptions, and own the result.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Map">Trace the source, decision rule, review boundary, handoff, and failure state.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose">Pick one loop whose source, owner, and result can be checked.</Step>
  <Step title="Run for one week">Connect the required systems and keep customer promises or system writes behind review.</Step>
  <Step title="Update with founders">Review misses and edge cases with Shubhranshu and Hemanth, then revise the workflow contract.</Step>
  <Step title="Measure">Compare the agreed baseline with response time, overdue work, SLA misses, review load, or the metric chosen for that workflow.</Step>
</Steps>
