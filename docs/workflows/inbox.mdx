Inbox keeps the source message at the center. ubik ranks the threads that need attention, preserves quoted history, and keeps context and actions close without replacing the conversation.

## The inbox loop

<Steps>
  <Step title="Triage the queue">Use Business critical, Action required, Waiting on, Updates, and All mail to narrow the work.</Step>
  <Step title="Read the thread">Check the current message and the conversation it responds to.</Step>
  <Step title="Inspect supporting context">Open summaries, tasks, files, links, or waiting dependencies only when needed.</Step>
  <Step title="Move the work">Reply, assign, create a task, ask ubik, or wait for the missing owner or evidence.</Step>
</Steps>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What ubik reads" icon="eye">
    Only approved inboxes and connected sources. Customer, supplier, pricing, and margin data does not train a model.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What remains human-reviewed" icon="user-check">
    Customer-facing replies, approvals, assignments, and system writebacks remain reviewable before they move.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
