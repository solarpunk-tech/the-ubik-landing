<Update label="October 15, 2026" description="Document Verification, rebuilt" tags={["Document Verification", "CEO Panel", "Capture", "Planning"]}>
  ## Document Verification

  - One approval surface: every document in a shipment, with its verdict and the reason, in one place.
  - A document-by-document workspace for checking each file against the PO.
  - Stricter verdicts: a document only passes when every check passes, and errors say what failed.
  - A settings dialog with **Access**, so admins choose who can verify and approve.

  ## CEO Panel

  - An ask bar on every view. Type a question to get an answer, or leave a comment for the team.

  ## Capture

  - Pick the report and the day first, then scan.

  ## Planning

  - Planning reports can arrive by email. Percentages in the Excel export are formatted correctly.

  ## Faster

  - Workflow pages load on their own, so the app opens quicker, and saving no longer waits for a full refresh.
</Update>

<Update label="October 13, 2026" description="Steadier sign-in, CEO Panel v5, packaging containers" tags={["Sign-in", "CEO Panel", "Packaging Tracker", "Production Reconciliation"]}>
  ## Sign-in

  - A slow connection no longer signs you out. ubik waits for the session check instead of treating it as expired.

  ## CEO Panel

  - Data-trust markers show how fresh each number is, plus inventory ageing.
  - Clicking a price row shows that item's lines.

  ## Packaging PO

  - Containers are redesigned. Inner and outer packs are separate, closing a container needs a deliberate switch, and you can add notes and filter.

  ## Production Reconciliation

  - Clicking a production date no longer blanks the page, and the screen responds faster.
</Update>

<Update label="October 10, 2026" description="Instant Packaging Tracker buttons" tags={["Packaging Tracker", "Mobile"]}>
  - Packaging Tracker buttons respond instantly, and only the row you changed refreshes.
  - Ask ubik in the iOS and Android apps: dictation works, and the layout stays put while you type.
</Update>

<Update label="October 9, 2026" description="Supplier weekly report and previous orders" tags={["Supplier Update", "Packaging Tracker", "CEO Panel"]}>
  ## Supplier Update

  - A **Weekly report** tab with an Excel download.
  - Notes on updates that arrive early, and the collection cutoff on every row.
  - Ask about any row. The separate Accept step is gone.

  ## Packaging PO

  - The previous order now shows under each line item, so you can check artwork against the last run.

  ## CEO Panel

  - A source that failed now says so, instead of showing "nothing to report".
</Update>

<Update label="October 6, 2026" description="Packaging Tracker: why this status" tags={["Packaging Tracker", "Replenishment", "CEO Panel", "Mobile"]}>
  ## Packaging Tracker

  - **Why this status**, in plain words, on every task.
  - A **Chase** status, and bulk close for tasks that are waiting.
  - **Done** lists closed tasks, with **Reopen**.

  ## Replenishment

  - Pick warehouses, customers and products by name.

  ## CEO Panel

  - A visible French / English switch in the panel header.

  ## Mobile

  - Dictation no longer crashes the iOS app.
</Update>
