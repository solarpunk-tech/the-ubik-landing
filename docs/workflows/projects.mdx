## What we are building

- Pin accounts, lanes, SKUs, suppliers, shipments, documents, and owners to a project.
- One memory scope across every meeting, task, thread, and decision the project touches.
- Shared with operators working on it; external collaborators can be looped in by email.
- A live brief showing what changed since last review, what is blocked, and what needs a decision.

Until Projects ships, use [Ask ubik](/workflows/ask-ubik) to assemble the same context across linked work.
