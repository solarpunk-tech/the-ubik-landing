Inbox is where most trade decisions still arrive — buyer RFQs, supplier replies, freight forwarder updates, FDA notices, margin escalations. ubik watches the inbox, ranks what needs you now, and pulls the trade context it already knows about the thread.

## What ubik does in your inbox

| Surface | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Operating queue | Threads that need a reply, approval, or handoff are promoted out of the noise. |
| Context recall | Past quotes, prior shipments, supplier history, margin guardrails — pulled into the reply panel before you open the thread. |
| Next-step draft | Reply, internal handoff, escalation, or a queued task — drafted but waiting for your approve / edit / reject. |
| Quiet routing | Newsletters, marketing, and low-signal threads stay where they are. ubik does not touch them. |

## A typical inbox loop

<div className="ubik-steps">
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>01 · Surface</span> ubik flags the threads where a decision is waiting and explains why.</div>
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>02 · Recall</span> The reply panel arrives pre-loaded with account history, inventory, margin guardrails, and lane history.</div>
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>03 · Decide</span> Approve the draft, edit it, assign it to a teammate, or reject it back to the queue.</div>
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>04 · Trail</span> Every reply leaves a reviewer, timestamp, and the source context the agent saw.</div>
</div>

<div className="ubik-panel">
  <strong>Privacy posture.</strong> ubik reads only the inboxes your admin has approved. Replies, approvals, and ERP handoffs stay human-reviewed. Your data does not train any model.
</div>

## Useful prompts inside Inbox

| Situation | Ask ubik |
| --- | --- |
| Backlog triage | "Which inbox threads have a buyer waiting more than 36 hours?" |
| Quote prep | "Pull the last three quotes for this account and the current margin guardrail." |
| Escalation | "Draft a partial-shipment reply to this buyer that keeps finance approval in the loop." |
| Manager review | "Which threads have been blocked the longest and who owns the next move?" |
