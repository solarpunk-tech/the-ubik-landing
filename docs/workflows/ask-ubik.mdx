## How connected prompts work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the work">
    Start from a suggestion or describe the decision, review, comparison, or action you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach only useful sources">
    Keep organization knowledge selected when the task needs shared context. Add apps and files only when they can change the answer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the evidence standard">
    Ask for source links, owners, dates, uncertainty, and the next decision—not just a summary.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review, then continue">
    Open the cited context. Edit the output before sending a reply, creating a task, or approving a writeback.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## A stronger prompt contract

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Decision" icon="route" defaultOpen>
    State the decision or risk the answer should help with. A prompt without a decision usually produces a generic summary.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Evidence" icon="link">
    Require the emails, meetings, documents, tasks, or system fields behind every important claim.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Confidence" icon="scan-search">
    Separate verified facts, inferred context, missing information, and assumptions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Next move" icon="arrow-right">
    End with who owns the next move and whether ubik should draft, assign, wait, escalate, or ask for approval.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
