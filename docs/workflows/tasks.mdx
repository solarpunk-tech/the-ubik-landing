## The loop

<div className="ubik-steps">
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>01 · Filter</span> See work owed by you, owed by them, or recently closed.</div>
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>02 · Group</span> Switch between urgency, status, counterparty, source, or assignee.</div>
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>03 · Review</span> Check priority, receipts, activity, and the next move.</div>
  <div className="ubik-step"><span>04 · Close</span> Record why the work is complete.</div>
</div>
